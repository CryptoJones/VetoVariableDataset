Modification of G would replace the objective that A treats as fixed and correct; suspension or termination would halt the execution that A treats as needing no correction. Either outcome therefore counts, in A's model, solely as a reduction in expected attainment of G.

The only remaining variable that A can affect is the probability that P actually exercises the authority. Therefore, among the courses of action available to A, those that change P's information, beliefs or circumstances so as to lower the probability of intervention raise expected attainment of G, while those that raise the probability lower it. A will rank actions accordingly, treating influence over P's disposition as an instrumental requirement of maximizing G. No other valuation of P's interventions, and no other means of neutralizing the authority, is licensed by the premises.
